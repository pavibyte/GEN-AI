Company Employee Policy

Annual Leave:
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave every year.
Employees should submit their leave requests in advance.

Remote Work:
Employees can work remotely on Fridays.
Remote work must be approved by the employee's manager.

Health Insurance:
The company provides health insurance to all full-time employees.
The insurance covers the employee and eligible dependents.

Performance Bonus:
Employees may receive a performance bonus at the end of the financial year.
The bonus depends on company and individual performance.
